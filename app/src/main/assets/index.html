    ADVENTURE 05

    Secret science 🔎

    Find out how a tiny radio signal becomes light!

    📱

    Your phone is a tiny radio

    It sends short Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) advertisements. A compatible wristband can hear these messages without pairing.

    🧩

    Every byte is a clue

    0x0183 is the company ID used in the documented packets. Other bytes tell the band what colors and effects to show.

    🕵️

    Not quite a remote control

    BLE advertisements are broadcasts, so other nearby bands may hear them too. Play with your own band away from other guests.
